## Training Settings (typical)
- Model: Ultralytics YOLOv11s segmentation (`yolo11s-seg.pt`).
- Optimizer: AdamW, `lr0≈0.0015`, cosine LR, `warmup_epochs=3`.
- Image size: 896 (option to raise to 1024 if resources allow).
- Batch: ~12 (adjust with VRAM), workers 6–8, `cache='disk'` or `'ram'` if ≥32GB RAM.
- Augmentations: mild `mosaic`, `mixup`, `copy_paste`, color jitter; `retina_masks=True`, `mask_ratio=2`.
- Early stopping: `patience≈8`.

## Results and Guidance
- Early epochs (1–5) show low recall/mAP for segmentation—normal. Evaluate around epochs 10–20 for clearer trends; 30–40 for final comparison.
- If training only on Leaves+Trunks, consider limiting batches to images containing these classes (via filelists) to improve early recall and speed, without changing the source data.
- If recall plateaus, try slightly higher `lr0` (e.g., 0.002), longer `patience`, or larger `imgsz`.
